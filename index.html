my research

Koop-Tech

Video-based speaking materials for senior high school

Kurikulum Merdeka, Phase E (Grade X)

Koop-Tech blends cooperative learning with technology to help students speak with more confidence. Short videos act as the input, followed by speaking practice, activity sheets, group discussion and a guide for teachers.

The materials target giving opinions, joining discussions, building arguments, short presentations, vocabulary and fluency. It is a Design and Development Research project, so the goal is to develop and validate the materials rather than test their effectiveness in an experiment.

  1. Needs analysis
  2. Product development
  3. Expert validation

Working title: The Development of Video-Based Koop-Tech Learning Materials to Support Students' Speaking Skills in Senior High School
